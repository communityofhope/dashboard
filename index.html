About the MAPS Framework
COH measures discipleship health through four stages of engagement: Disinterested → reaching those outside the church, Interested → engaging new visitors, Growing Together → developing committed disciples, Fully Devoted → equipping core leaders.

🌍
Disinterested
Has a person responded to our reach towards them?
👋
Interested
Has a person taken a step towards us?
🌱
Growing Together
Has a person taken a step of commitment?
🔥
Fully Devoted
Has a person placed themselves in a relationship where they are spiritually, relationally and missionally accountable?
